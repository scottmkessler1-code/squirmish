Caterpillar Continental Combat

Pass-and-play prototype. Pick 2 to 6 players.

Left/Right walk · Up/Down aim · Enter jump · Keys 1–5 or click a weapon · hold Space to charge, release to fire (shotgun and dynamite fire instantly; airstrike lands farther with more charge). Walk over crates to restock ammo. Each player has two worms; last team standing wins.
