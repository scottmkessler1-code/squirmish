Caterpillar Continental Combat

Left/Right walk · Up/Down aim · Enter jump · Keys 1–6 or click a weapon · hold Space to charge, release to fire (shotgun and punch fire instantly; tap Space to drop dynamite at your feet or charge to throw it; cluster bursts on impact into bomblets; airstrike lands farther with more charge). After firing you have 3 seconds to walk away. Walk over crates to restock ammo. Each team has two caterpillars; last team standing wins.
